### The scope of control, administrative capabilities, and oversight responsibilities.
### Remember that following is
Senior Department Head
*   **Position:** Second in command, overseeing a major departmental segment.
*   **Justification:**
    *   **Departmental Scope:** Possesses administrative control over a large segment of the organization, typically encompassing multiple sub-departments or teams.
    *   **Sub-User Management:** Can manage (create, edit, deactivate) users within their specific department(s), limited to roles below them (Department Heads, Employees).
    *   **Departmental Structure:** Defines and manages folder structures and default permissions for their entire department.
    *   **Workflow Oversight:** Monitors and initiates departmental workflows, with the authority to approve high-level steps.
    *   **Broad Document Access:** Full access to all documents and folders within their departmental purview, but not across the entire organization.
*   **Key Capabilities:** `Departmental User Management`, `Departmental Permissions`, `Workflow Oversight`, `Broad Departmental Access`.

### Sidebar Options Content Detail for DOCUMENT SOLUTIONS
### This is the recommended content for each sidebar options
### THIS IS ONLY FOR Senior Department Head Sidebar Options Content

*   **Dashboard (Departmental Dashboard/Overview):**
    *   **Purpose:** Overview of their department's document activity and status.
    *   **Content:**
        *   Departmental Document Statistics (Total documents, recent uploads).
        *   Active/Pending Workflows for their department.
        *   Overview of department users' activity.
        *   Quick links to departmental files and user management.

    NAVIGATION
*   **Files (Browse and manage all documents/folders in their domain):**
    *   **Purpose:** Access to all documents and folders within their assigned departments, with elevated management capabilities.
    *   **Content:** Similar to Admin's "Files" but scoped to their departmental hierarchy.
        *   Hierarchical Folder Tree/View: Showing folders they oversee.
        *   Document Listing: Table of documents within selected folder.
            *   Columns: Name, Type, Uploader, Last Modified, Current Version, Lock Status, Permissions Icon.
        *   Search & Filter: Full-text search, filter by metadata (type, tags, uploader, date range).
        *   Actions: Create/Delete/Rename Folder, Upload Document, View/Edit Document, Delete Document, Manage Permissions (for selected file/folder within their domain), Force Unlock Document, View Version History.

*   **Department Users (Manage users within their department):**
    *   **Purpose:** Oversee and manage users belonging to their department.
    *   **Content:**
        *   User List: Table of users within their department.
            *   Columns: Name, Email, Role, Last Login, Status.
        *   Search & Filter: By name, email, role.
        *   Actions: Create New User (within their department's scope), Edit User Details (Name, Email, Password, Role (restricted to lower roles), Activate/Deactivate User.

*   **Workflows (Monitor/initiate departmental workflows):**
    *   **Purpose:** Oversee and manage workflows relevant to their department.
    *   **Content:**
        *   Workflow Template List: View available workflow templates.
        *   Active Workflows: List of workflow instances running within their department, with status.
        *   Actions: Initiate a New Workflow, Approve/Reject workflow steps.
        *   Workflow Audit: View history of specific workflow instances.

*   **Shared (Documents shared with/by them):**
    *   **Purpose:** Centralized view of documents involved in sharing.
    *   **Content:**
        *   "Shared By Me": List of documents/folders they have shared, with who and permissions.
        *   "Shared With Me": List of documents/folders shared with them, with who shared it and permissions.
        *   Actions: Revoke sharing, Change permissions on shared items.

*   **Notifications (Notifications):**
    *   **Purpose:** Receive timely alerts about system events relevant to their role.
    *   **Content:**
        *   Chronological list of notifications (e.g., "Document X updated by Y", "Workflow Z requires your approval", "New user added to your department").
        *   Actions: Mark as Read, Delete Notification, Click to navigate to relevant item.
