### The scope of control, administrative capabilities, and oversight responsibilities.
### Remember that following is
Department Head
*   **Position:** Manages a specific team or sub-department.
*   **Justification:**
    *   **Team/Sub-Department Scope:** Direct administrative control over a smaller, focused group of employees.
    *   **Employee Management:** Manages (creates, edits) users within their *direct reporting structure* or sub-department, limited to the 'Employee' role.
    *   **Folder Ownership/Management:** Primarily responsible for creating, organizing, and managing documents and folders relevant to their team's operations.
    *   **Workflow Participation & Initiation:** Actively participates in and initiates workflows pertinent to their team's documents, often being a key approver for team-level tasks.
    *   **Granular Permission Setting:** Can set specific permissions for their team members on documents/folders they own or manage.
*   **Key Capabilities:** `Team User Management`, `Folder & Document Management (Team Level)`, `Workflow Participation`, `Granular Team Permissions`.

### Sidebar Options Content Detail for DOCUMENT SOLUTIONS
### This is the recommended content for each sidebar options
### THIS IS ONLY FOR Department Head Sidebar Options Content

*   **Dashboard (Departmental Dashboard/Overview):**
    *   **Purpose:** Quick overview of their sub-department's document activity.
    *   **Content:**
        *   Recent Activity (uploads, edits in their sub-department).
        *   Documents awaiting their approval in workflows.
        *   Quick links to files and team management.

    NAVIGATION
*   **Files (Browse and manage documents/folders within their sub-department):**
    *   **Purpose:** Primary access point for documents they manage.
    *   **Content:** Similar to Sr. Dept Head's "Files" but scoped to their sub-department/assigned folders.
        *   Hierarchical Folder Tree/View: For their accessible folders.
        *   Document Listing: Table of documents.
            *   Columns: Name, Type, Uploader, Last Modified, Current Version, Lock Status.
        *   Search & Filter: Full-text search, filter by metadata.
        *   Actions: Create/Delete/Rename Folder, Upload Document, View/Edit Document, Delete Document (permissions permitting), Manage Permissions (for selected file/folder), Lock/Unlock Document, View Version History.

*   **Upload (Upload new documents):**
    *   **Purpose:** Dedicated interface for adding new documents.
    *   **Content:**
        *   Drag-and-drop area for files.
        *   File selection button.
        *   Dropdown to select target folder.
        *   Input fields for initial metadata (title, tags, type, correspondent).
        *   Upload progress indicator.

*   **Team (Manage employees in their sub-department, specific permissions):**
    *   **Purpose:** Basic management of direct reports or team members.
    *   **Content:**
        *   Employee List: Table of employees under their direct management.
            *   Columns: Name, Email, Last Login.
        *   Actions: View Employee Profile, Set specific permissions for team members on certain files/folders (within the Dept Head's own permission scope).

*   **Workflows (Monitor/participate in relevant workflows):**
    *   **Purpose:** Engage with and monitor document workflows they are part of.
    *   **Content:**
        *   "My Pending Actions": List of workflow steps awaiting their review/approval.
        *   "My Initiated Workflows": List of workflows they started, with their current status.
        *   Actions: Perform workflow actions (Approve, Reject, Request Changes) directly from the list or a dedicated workflow detail page.

*   **Shared (Documents shared with/by them):**
    *   **Purpose:** View and manage documents shared with them or by them.
    *   **Content:** Same as Senior Department Head's "Shared" section.

*   **Notifications (Notifications):**
    *   **Purpose:** Receive alerts about relevant document and workflow activities.
    *   **Content:** Same as Senior Department Head's "Notifs" section.
