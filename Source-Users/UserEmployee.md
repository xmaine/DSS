### The scope of control, administrative capabilities, and oversight responsibilities.
### Remember that following is
Employee
*   **Position:** End-user, consumer, and creator of documents within their assigned scope.
*   **Justification:**
    *   **Task-Oriented:** Focuses on day-to-day document-related tasks assigned to them.
    *   **Restricted Access:** Access to documents and folders is strictly defined by permissions granted by higher-level roles.
    *   **Document Creation/Editing:** Primary creator of new documents and editor of documents they are assigned to work on.
    *   **Workflow Participant:** Performs specific actions within workflows (e.g., review, feedback, upload required documents).
    *   **Collaboration Tools:** Utilizes features like annotations, comments, and sharing (within their permitted scope).
*   **Key Capabilities:** `Document Creation/Upload`, `View/Edit Assigned Documents`, `Workflow Task Execution`, `Basic Collaboration`.

### Sidebar Options Content Detail for DOCUMENT SOLUTIONS
### This is the recommended content for each sidebar options
### THIS IS ONLY FOR Employee Sidebar Options Content

#### Dashboard (Departmental Dashboard/Overview):**
    *   **Purpose:** Quick overview of employee's activity.

    NAVIGATION
*   **Files (Browse and access allowed documents/folders):**
    *   **Purpose:** Primary access point for all documents they have permission to view.
    *   **Content:**
        *   Hierarchical Folder Tree/View: Only showing folders they have access to.
        *   Document Listing: Table of documents within selected folder.
            *   Columns: Name, Type, Uploader, Last Modified, Lock Status.
        *   Search & Filter: Full-text search, filter by metadata.
        *   Actions: View Document, Edit Document (if permitted and not locked), Lock/Unlock Document (if permitted), Add Annotation, View Version History.

*   **Upload (Upload new documents):**
    *   **Purpose:** Dedicated interface for adding new documents to permitted folders.
    *   **Content:** Same as Department Head's "Upload" section.

*   **My Documents (Documents they own/uploaded):**
    *   **Purpose:** Quick access to documents they personally created or are designated as owner.
    *   **Content:**
        *   Document Listing: Table of documents where the current user is the uploader or designated owner.
            *   Columns: Name, Folder, Last Modified, Lock Status.
        *   Search & Filter: Limited to their own documents.
        *   Actions: View/Edit/Delete (if permitted), Share, Lock/Unlock, View Version History.

*   **Shared (Documents shared with them):**
    *   **Purpose:** View documents that have been specifically shared with them.
    *   **Content:**
        *   "Shared With Me": List of documents/folders shared with them, with who shared it and permissions.
        *   Actions: View Document, Edit Document (if permitted), Add Annotation.

*   **Workflows (View/participate in assigned workflows):**
    *   **Purpose:** Participate in workflows where they are assigned a task.
    *   **Content:**
        *   "My Pending Actions": List of workflow steps awaiting their action.
        *   "My Workflows": List of workflows they are currently involved in or have initiated.
        *   Actions: Perform assigned workflow actions (e.g., Review, Provide Feedback, Submit for Approval).

*   **Notifications (Notifications):**
    *   **Purpose:** Receive alerts about relevant document and workflow activities.
    *   **Content:** Same as Senior Department Head's "Notifications" section.
