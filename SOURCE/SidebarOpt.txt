### Sidebar Options by User Role for DOCUMENT SOLUTIONS

**General Principles:**
*   Options are visible only if the user role has permission to access the underlying functionality.
*   Prioritize common actions for each role.
*   "Home" or "Dash" (Dashboard) is assumed for all users as a landing page.

---

#### 1. System Administrator Sidebar

*   **Home/Dashboard** (Dashboard/Overview)
*   **Files** (Browse and manage all documents/folders)
*   **Users** (Manage user accounts, roles, groups)
*   **Types** (Manage Document Types, Correspondents)
*   **Perms** (Manage Global Permissions & Access Control)
*   **Workflows** (Define and manage workflow templates)
*   **Logs** (System Audit Logs)
*   **Config** (System Configuration & Settings)

#### 2. Senior Department Head Sidebar

*   **Home/Dashboard** (Departmental Dashboard/Overview)
*   **Files** (Browse and manage all documents/folders in their domain)
*   **DeptUsers** (Manage users within their department)
*   **Workflows** (Monitor/initiate departmental workflows)
*   **Shared** (Documents shared with/by them)
*   **Notifs** (Notifications)

#### 3. Department Head Sidebar

*   **Home/Dashboard** (Departmental Dashboard/Overview)
*   **Files** (Browse and manage documents/folders within their sub-department)
*   **Upload** (Upload new documents)
*   **Team** (Manage employees in their sub-department, specific permissions)
*   **Workflows** (Monitor/participate in relevant workflows)
*   **Shared** (Documents shared with/by them)
*   **Notifs** (Notifications)

#### 4. Employee Sidebar

*   **Home/Dashboard** (Dashboard/Overview)
*   **Files** (Browse and access allowed documents/folders)
*   **Upload** (Upload new documents)
*   **MyDocs** (Documents they own/uploaded)
*   **Shared** (Documents shared with them)
*   **Workflows** (View/participate in assigned workflows)
*   **Notifs** (Notifications)

---

This structure ensures that each user role sees a streamlined set of options relevant to their responsibilities, enhancing usability and reducing clutter.
